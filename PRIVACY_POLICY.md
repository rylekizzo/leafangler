# Privacy Policy for LeafAngler

**Effective Date: [Current Date]**

## Overview
LeafAngler ("the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This privacy policy explains how we handle information when you use our leaf angle measurement application.

## Information Collection and Use

### Data We Access
The App accesses the following device features to provide its functionality:

1. **Motion Sensors** (Gyroscope & Accelerometer)
   - Used exclusively to calculate leaf angles and orientations
   - Data is processed locally on your device
   - No motion data is transmitted or stored externally

2. **Location Services** (GPS)
   - Used to record geographic coordinates with measurements when permitted
   - Location data is stored locally on your device only
   - GPS access is optional and can be denied without affecting core functionality

### Data Storage
- All measurement data is stored locally on your device
- No user data is transmitted to external servers
- No analytics or tracking services are used
- No personal information is collected or stored

### Data Sharing
- The App does not share any data with third parties
- Export functionality allows you to manually share your data via:
  - CSV file export to your chosen destination
  - iOS native share sheet (under your control)
- You maintain complete control over if, when, and how your data is shared

## Data Security
- All data remains on your device using iOS local storage
- Data is protected by your device's security measures
- No cloud synchronization or backup services are used
- Deleting the App removes all associated data from your device

## Children's Privacy
The App does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. The App is designed for use by researchers, students, and professionals in botanical and agricultural fields.

## Your Rights
You have complete control over your data:
- View all stored measurements within the App
- Export your data at any time
- Delete individual recordings or entire datasets
- Clear all data through the Settings menu
- Remove all data by uninstalling the App

## Changes to This Policy
We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the App Store listing and on our website.

## No Third-Party Services
The App does not use:
- Analytics services
- Advertising networks
- Crash reporting tools
- Cloud storage services
- User accounts or authentication
- Social media integration

## Data Retention
- Data is retained only on your device
- You control when data is deleted
- Uninstalling the App removes all data
- No backups are maintained by the App developer

LeafAngler is a privacy-focused scientific tool. Your measurements and location data never leave your device unless you explicitly choose to export them. We don't collect, store, or have access to any of your data.
